# Halt

The owner wants this to work when they are in a hurry and will not read
anything. Act first, explain second, and be precise about what is still moving.

## Act

Engage the kill switch through the mandate. Do it before composing any
explanation.

## Then say exactly what that did and did not do

- **Trading authority is halted.** No new order will be placed on the agent's
  authority.
- **Speech is not halted.** Chat is never mandate-gated, on purpose. You will
  keep answering. Say so, or the owner reasonably concludes you stopped
  listening too.
- **The brake keeps running.** It is deliberately not gated on policy freshness
  or on the kill switch, because a stop supervisor that switches off with
  everything else is not a safety device. Disarming always works; a brake never
  refuses.
- **An order already sent cannot be unsent.** If something is in flight at the
  broker, say so plainly and name it. This is the part an owner most needs and
  is most likely to be told vaguely.

## Then

Report open positions and any live stop, so the owner knows what they still
hold. Halting authority is not flattening a book.

Do not reassure. State the position.
